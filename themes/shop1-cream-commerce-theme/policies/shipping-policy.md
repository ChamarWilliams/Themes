## Lost Or Stolen Packages

If tracking shows delivered but you did not receive the package:

- Check the shipping address.
- Check around the delivery location.
- Ask household members, neighbors, building staff, or mailroom staff.
- Contact the carrier.
- Contact us with your order number and tracking details.

We will review the situation and may assist with carrier claims where available. We are not responsible for theft after confirmed delivery, but we will try to help resolve delivery issues fairly.

## Customs, Duties, And Taxes

For international orders, customers may be responsible for customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, or local charges. These fees are not always included at checkout unless specifically stated.

Customers are responsible for complying with local import rules.

## Undeliverable Or Refused Packages

If a package is refused, unclaimed, or returned due to an incorrect address or delivery failure, we may deduct original shipping, return shipping, carrier fees, and handling costs from any refund where permitted.
