# Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how Shop1 processes, ships, tracks, and handles delivery issues for online orders.

## Order Processing

Orders are processed after payment confirmation, fraud review, and supplier or fulfillment availability checks.

Standard processing time is usually 2 to 5 business days. Processing can take longer during holidays, high order volume, inventory checks, supplier delays, address review, payment review, or product-specific handling.

Orders placed on weekends or holidays may begin processing on the next business day.

## Delivery Estimates

Standard estimated delivery time is usually 7 to 20 business days after processing.

Delivery times shown on product pages, checkout, messages, emails, advertisements, or order updates are estimates unless expressly stated as guaranteed.

Because Shop1 may use supplier or fulfillment partner shipping, delivery time can vary by item, destination, carrier, customs review, and local delivery conditions.

## Shipping Locations

Shop1 is configured for United States orders first.

International shipping may be added later. If international checkout is available, customers may be responsible for customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, local charges, and compliance with local import rules unless checkout specifically states otherwise.

Some products may be unavailable in certain states, territories, countries, regions, PO boxes, military addresses, remote locations, or restricted destinations due to carrier limits, supplier restrictions, legal restrictions, or product restrictions.

## Tracking

When tracking is available, tracking information will be sent through Shopify order updates, email, SMS if enabled, or the order status page.

Tracking may take several business days to update after a label is created. Some economy or supplier-direct shipments may have limited tracking detail.

## Split Shipments

If an order contains multiple items, items may ship separately from different locations. You may receive more than one package, tracking number, or delivery date.

## Delays

Delays may happen due to supplier processing, carrier delays, customs, weather, high order volume, holidays, incorrect addresses, payment review, fraud review, regional disruptions, or events outside our direct control.

If we cannot ship within the stated timeframe, we will notify you and provide options as required, which may include consenting to the delay or canceling for a refund.

## Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address at checkout.

If you entered the wrong address, contact us immediately through Shopify Inbox. We cannot guarantee changes after an order has been processed, submitted to a supplier, packed, labeled, or shipped.

If a package is delayed, lost, returned, refused, or undeliverable because of an incorrect address, missing apartment/unit number, inaccessible delivery location, refusal, or failure to pick up the package, we may be unable to refund the order.

## Lost Or Stolen Packages

If tracking shows delivered but you did not receive the package:

- Check the delivery address on the order.
- Check around the delivery location.
- Ask household members, neighbors, building staff, front desk staff, or mailroom staff.
- Contact the carrier.
- Contact us through Shopify Inbox with your order number and tracking details.

We will review the situation and may assist with carrier claims where available. We are not responsible for theft after confirmed delivery, but we will try to help resolve delivery issues fairly.

## Damaged, Wrong, Missing, Or Incomplete Shipments

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, incomplete, missing, or materially different from the listing, contact us through Shopify Inbox as soon as possible.

Include your order number, product name, tracking number, clear description of the issue, and photos or video of the item, packaging, shipping label, and issue.

Approved cases may be resolved with a replacement, refund, partial refund, store credit, discount code, or another reasonable resolution. We may resolve approved cases without requiring a physical return.

## Undeliverable Or Refused Packages

If a package is refused, unclaimed, returned, abandoned, or undeliverable due to an incorrect address or delivery failure, we may deduct original shipping, return shipping, carrier fees, supplier fees, and handling costs from any refund where permitted.

## Contact

For shipping questions, use Shopify Inbox through the chat or contact option shown on the store.
