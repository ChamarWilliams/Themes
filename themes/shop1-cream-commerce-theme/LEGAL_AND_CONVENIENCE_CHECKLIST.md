# Legal And Convenience Checklist

This Shopify website theme includes:

- Optional account links
- Guest-friendly cart flow
- Category drawer
- Footer policy links
- Cookie notice
- Starter policy page templates

Before launch, configure Shopify Admin policies with your final business details:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Refund/Return Policy
- Shipping Policy
- Contact page

Recommended defaults:

- Keep customer login optional.
- Keep guest checkout enabled.
- Offer eligible 30-day returns.
- Use estimated delivery language.
- Add a real support email.
- Review all policy text before taking real orders.

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.
